01

Rocket camera mount.

Designer · Oct 2026 to present · SAME Structures · UW Tacoma

Key result 4 in body tube · camera

An external mount that holds a camera on the outside of the team rocket, so the flight can be recorded and checked. The hard part is shape: anything that sticks out catches air, so the mount has to stay aerodynamic. The current version is a single low wedge that hugs the body tube, with the lens looking along the tube. Designed and revised in Onshape with the SAME structures team at UW Tacoma.
